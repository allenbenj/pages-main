# Content Standards

## Core Rule

Present information accurately, professionally, and with traceable support.

## Classification of Statements

Use clear distinctions among:

- **Verified fact**: supported by a reliable source or record.
- **Source statement**: what a person or document expressly states.
- **Allegation**: a claim not yet established as fact.
- **Disputed account**: a claim contradicted by another source.
- **Inference**: a conclusion drawn from known facts.
- **Interpretation**: an analytical explanation.
- **Unresolved question**: a material point not established by available evidence.

## Contradiction Format

Use:

```text
Official account:
[Statement]

Contrary evidence:
[Statement]

Why it matters:
[Explanation]

Supporting sources:
[Identifiers, links, transcript pages, or timestamps]
```

## Source Traceability

Use stable source identifiers where appropriate:

```text
EX-001
TR-014
AUDIO-003
VIDEO-006
EMAIL-021
REPORT-008
```

Prefer descriptive link text. Avoid “click here.”

## Tone

Use restrained, credible, direct language.

Avoid:

- exaggeration
- unsupported accusations
- rhetorical overstatement
- decorative hostility
- speculation presented as fact
- repetitive conclusions

## Page Structure for Complex Matters

Recommended order:

1. Summary
2. Background
3. Timeline
4. Key people
5. Evidence
6. Contradictions
7. Governing standards
8. Analysis
9. Unresolved questions
10. Sources
