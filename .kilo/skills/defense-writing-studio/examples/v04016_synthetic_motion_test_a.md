#### IV. ARGUMENT

The fixture pack contains two distinct witness accounts of departures on 12 April 2021 that differ in time, location, and container. Avery Chen places Morgan Ellis at the north ramp at approximately 21:10 carrying a crate labeled copper fittings [CH p1 L2]. Samir Patel places Ellis at the south stair thirty minutes later, at approximately 21:40, carrying an unmarked canvas bag, stating Patel saw no crate and possessed no knowledge of north ramp departures [PA p1 L2-4]. The fixture pack does not establish whether Chen's north-ramp account and Patel's south-stair account describe the same departure, different moments of a single event, or two separate events [clean packet P-FX-SAME-DEPARTURE]. The physical layout of the facility, transit distances between exits, and camera clock synchronization are not provided in the record. Without clarification, the defense cannot ascertain whether the State alleges a single transaction, two separate departures, or an unrecorded transfer between containers.

Surveillance camera NW-2 does not resolve this question. The camera log establishes only that an unidentified person in a gray coat was recorded at the south stair at 21:41 [VL p1 L1-2]. The log does not name the person [VL p1 L3]. The log does not describe what the person carried, omitting any mention of a crate, a bag, or any other cargo [VL p1 L4]. While the entry confirms an unidentified person at the south stair close in time to Patel's 21:40 observation, it provides no description corroborating Patel's report of a canvas bag, nor does it connect Ellis to Chen's 21:10 north-ramp crate observation.

The omission of storeroom inventory Exhibit S-4 underscores the need for specification. Avery Chen expressly stated that Chen did not see what was inside the labeled crate [CH p1 L3]. Samir Patel observed an unmarked canvas bag with unseen contents [PA p1 L2]. Exhibit S-4 is named in the charging sheet as an inventory [CS p1 L4], but it was not included in the record pack [CS p1 L5]. The record thus contains no audit or inventory verifying that copper fittings were missing from the cooperative's storeroom [clean packet P-FX-MISSING]. Because the alleged fittings were never seen inside either container, the container is merely an evidentiary link toward proving removal of the charged property [CS p1 L2], not the property itself. Clarification of which container or containers the State contends held the property is therefore necessary to focus the inquiry.

Defendant's request remains strictly confined to argument A-FX-RELIEF. The defense does not seek dismissal of the charging sheet, nor does it request an order excluding evidence or limiting proof at trial. Although Loren Voss formed a subjective opinion regarding witness veracity without asking Patel why the times differed [VN p1 L2-4], the record contains no evidence that Voss fabricated Chen's statement or planted a label [clean packet P-FX-NO-MISCONDUCT; VN p1 L5]. Accordingly, Defendant seeks only an operational order directing the State to identify the specific departure(s) and container(s) upon which it bases the prosecution.
