# Expert cross-examination outline

Use this reference for an expert witness. It supplements `references/examination-outline-template.md`; do not write a competing general outline.

## Foundation

For each question, record:

- the topic;
- the question;
- the expected answer;
- the source pin (report page, transcript page and line, exhibit, or data file);
- the purpose;
- the fallback if the answer is unexpected.

## Topics

### 1. Qualification fit

- What is the witness's training and experience in the precise method?
- Has the witness performed this method on this type of item?
- What is the basis for the claimed expertise?

### 2. Personal work

- What did the witness personally do?
- What did someone else do?
- What did the witness review, and when?

### 3. Reliance on others

- Which statements, worksheets, or conclusions came from another analyst?
- Does the witness know the underlying data independently?
- What did the witness verify?

### 4. Method and validation

- What method was used?
- Was it validated for this use?
- What are its limits?

### 5. Data and assumptions

- What data were used?
- What assumptions were made?
- What would change the result?

### 6. Limits and overstatement

- Does the report distinguish a source-level association from an activity or offense conclusion?
- Does it state what the method does not establish?
- Does it use "consistent with" as if it meant "proves"?

### 7. Missing or withheld materials

- What file, data, or record was not reviewed?
- What would it have shown?
- Was the witness's opinion formed without it?

### 8. Controls, contamination, and proficiency

- What controls were run?
- What contamination indicators were checked?
- What is the witness's proficiency history on this method?

### 9. Contextual information

- What case information did the witness receive, and when?
- Did the interpretation change after the exposure?
- What did the witness record about the change?

### 10. Concessions

- What does the opinion not establish?
- What alternative explanation is consistent with the data?
- What would the witness need to see to change the opinion?

## Impeachment sequence

For a contradiction, use the `transcript-impeachment` commit, credit, confront, and control steps:

1. commit the witness to the current answer;
2. credit the prior statement and its source;
3. confront with the exact words and pin;
4. control the follow-up and preserve the record.

Do not fabricate an admission. Do not quote a summary as if it were the original. Do not ask one question too many after a useful concession.

## Rules

- Every question must be supported by the record or clearly exploratory.
- Every quotation must match the original character for character.
- No numeric probability or score in a court-facing outline.
- Preserve objections and rulings.
- Keep the expert's conclusion separate from the underlying facts.
- Route a scientific-method issue to `forensic-evidence-evaluation` and the claim register.

## Output

A topic outline, the questions, the expected answers, the source pins, the purpose, the fallback, the concessions sought, and the expected objections with responses.
