## Communication checklist

- States only terms the client authorized.
- Contains no bottom line, no unsupported fact, and no unverified authority (R9, K6).
- Privilege, confidentiality, and admissibility questions checked with `ethics-privilege-guardrails`.
- Tone professional and firm; no threats and no misrepresentation.
- Named person approved sending (K13).
