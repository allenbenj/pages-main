# Framing catalog: questions, not canned findings

These frames are analytical starting points, not factual findings or preapproved legal grounds. Use one only if source-supported and suited to the verified law and posture.

### Failure of proof
**Question:** Which charged element depends on a single uncorroborated inference or a materially disputed account? **Claim:** The evidence fails the verified sufficiency standard *only if the correct review standard is met*. **Avoid:** Saying every inconsistency defeats sufficiency, treating absence of corroboration as a universal legal requirement. **Draft:** Describe the exact element, what evidence is offered for it, and why the evidence might or might not support a reasonable finding.

### Independent objective evidence
**Question:** What does timestamped physical/recorded evidence actually show, and which account does it test? **Claim:** Specific portion of an account conflicts with independent footage or records. **Avoid:** Claiming the entire allegation is false because a peripheral timeline statement differs. **Draft:** Identify both precise accounts, exhibit, timestamp, and legal significance.

### Investigative omission
**Question:** What was not done, when the opportunity existed, what policy/practice applies, and which legal element or alternative remained untested? **Claim:** The record cannot support an investigative inference as strongly as the prosecution suggests. **Avoid:** "Not collected therefore exculpatory", "not interviewed therefore conspiracy". **Draft:** omission → standard/practice verified or unverified → lost test → concrete motion/discovery step.

### Conflicted or biased investigator
**Question:** What relationship existed, when known, how it might have affected investigative decisions, and what disclosures or substitutions were required under applicable policy or law? **Claim:** The connection creates an impeachment and reliability question. **Avoid:** Automatically converting family association into criminal misconduct or per se disqualification. **Draft:** Tie relationship to identifiable acts or omissions and request testimony, records or remedies proportionate to the showing.

### Inconsistent witness accounts
**Question:** Did a material act, sequence or circumstance change, and what explains that change? **Claim:** A precise inconsistency bears on reliability and the weight of that testimony. **Avoid:** Treating normal recall variation, trauma, or intoxication as categorical proof of deception. **Draft:** quote both versions fully enough to preserve context, identify a concrete legal consequence, consider plausible benign explanations.

### Prosecutorial representations / disclosure
**Question:** What was represented to court or defense, when, what underlying record was known, and what corrective steps occurred? **Claim:** A supported material misrepresentation or disclosure deficiency warrants specified judicial examination or relief under verified law. **Avoid:** Describing erroneous statements as knowing lies without proof of knowledge, or invoking Brady/Giglio/Napue without individual prerequisites and materiality.

### Timing and chronology
**Question:** Which timestamps are direct recording clocks, reported estimates, metadata artifacts, or later testimony? **Claim:** A verified mismatch changes the sequence, available opportunity, materiality or witness reliability. **Avoid:** Calling a device clock accurate without sync/source check. **Draft:** state clock basis, differences, correction, alternative offset and consequence.

### Physical mechanics / medical findings
**Question:** Which measurable physical claims have trustworthy evidence, and how do alternative positions or motions affect feasibility? **Claim:** The proposed sequence lacks adequate demonstrated support or requires further testing. **Avoid:** Unsupported biomechanical impossibility or "no injuries" assertions. **Draft:** cite dimensions, evidence scope and limitations; reserve expert conclusions.

### Forensic interpretation
**Question:** Does the lab report show an exact profile identification, partial/mixture association, exclusion or merely no interpretable result? **Claim:** Interpretation must match method and limits. **Avoid:** Overstating uniqueness or equating "not detected" with absolute absence. **Draft:** the report's actual observation, limitations, alternate sources, and independent testing needs.

### Competing explanation / investigative tunnel vision
**Question:** Were credible alternatives considered and tested? **Claim:** Identified one-directional choices can diminish reliability when documented. **Avoid:** Concluding confirmation bias equals intentional fabrication or automatic suppression. **Draft:** decision, known competing explanation, unperformed discriminating test, effect on evidentiary confidence, legal vehicle.

### Relief discipline
Pair **reliability problems** with cross-examination / expert testimony / appropriate instructions; **discovery gaps** with production / in-camera review / preservation; **constitutional violations** with doctrine-specific remedies and burdens; **procedural error** with timely objection / hearing / reconsideration / appeal. An allegation of unethical conduct does not automatically establish the predicate for vacatur, dismissal or exclusion.
