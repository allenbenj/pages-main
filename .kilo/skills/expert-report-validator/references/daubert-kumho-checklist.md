# Daubert / Kumho Checklist

Use this checklist when reviewing an expert report. Mark each item Present / Partial / Absent / Not Applicable and cite the report page or paragraph.

## Qualifications
- [ ] Education, training, or experience specific to the opinion offered.
- [ ] Prior testimony or publications on the same subject (if relevant).
- [ ] Any limitations or exclusions in prior cases noted.

## Reliability Factors (adapt to jurisdiction)
- [ ] Methodology can be (and has been) tested.
- [ ] Subjected to peer review and publication.
- [ ] Known or potential rate of error stated.
- [ ] Standards controlling the technique exist and were followed.
- [ ] Generally accepted in the relevant scientific or technical community.
- [ ] For non-scientific experts: other indicia of reliability (Kumho) identified.

## Application to Case Facts
- [ ] Facts and data considered are disclosed.
- [ ] Principles and methods are explained.
- [ ] How the methods were applied to the specific facts of this case is shown.
- [ ] No unsupported analytical leap from data to conclusion.
- [ ] Alternative explanations considered or ruled out (where expected).

## Disclosure Compliance (Fed. R. Civ. P. 26(a)(2)(B) or state equivalent)
- [ ] Complete statement of all opinions and bases.
- [ ] Facts or data considered.
- [ ] Exhibits that will be used.
- [ ] Qualifications (including publications and prior testimony list).
- [ ] Compensation.
- [ ] Signed by the expert.

## Common Red Flags
- Opinion stated as fact without methodological support.
- Reliance on facts not in the record or not disclosed.
- Methodology that cannot be replicated or tested from the report.
- Error rate or uncertainty omitted when the field normally reports it.
- Opinion that answers the ultimate legal question without proper framing.

## Output Notes
Record the page/paragraph for every “Present” or “Partial.”  
Any “Absent” on a core reliability or disclosure item is a potential motion point.
