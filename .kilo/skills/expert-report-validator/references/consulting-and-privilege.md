# Consulting experts, privilege, and work product

A consulting label is a fact about the role, not a legal conclusion about disclosure. A testifying label is also not a conclusion. No automatic disclosure follows from a consulting label, and no automatic protection follows from it either. Identify the verified trigger.

## Role analysis

| Question | Consulting | Testifying / anticipated testifying |
|---|---|---|
| What is the expert asked to do? | Advise counsel, test theories, review the file. | Offer an opinion in a report, deposition, or testimony. |
| Is a report required? | Usually not. | Verify the report and disclosure rule for the forum. |
| Is privilege or work product available? | Potentially; verify the jurisdiction and the specific communication. | The protection may be reduced or lost; verify the trigger. |
| What happens on a role change? | The disclosure consequence may change. | Confirm the report and disclosure before testimony. |
| What must be recorded? | The role, the scope, the privilege basis, and the trigger analysis. | The report, the disclosure status, and the testimony scope. |

## Work product and privilege

- Identify the document, communication, or draft at issue.
- Identify the claimed protection and its factual basis.
- Identify the verified rule and the exception.
- Do not reveal privileged content in a log or a summary.
- Do not treat a privilege claim as established merely because it is asserted.
- Do not treat a consulting label as an automatic shield.

## Funding and access

- For an indigent criminal defendant, identify the verified route for expert assistance and any ex parte procedure.
- *Ake v. Oklahoma* is a candidate authority (`US-AKE`, `UNVERIFIED`); verify the opinion, the standard, and the NC implementation route.
- Do not assume a funding request is granted or that a particular ex parte route exists.
- Record the request, the order, the funding source, and the scope.

## Ethics guardrails

- Follow `ethics-privilege-guardrails` and the applicable rules of professional conduct.
- Do not disclose confidential client information without a verified basis.
- Do not contact a represented person or a protected witness without the required authorization.
- Do not use a consulting expert to create a sham opinion or to hide a testifying opinion.

## Output

A role-and-privilege record with the role, the scope, the claimed protection, the verified trigger, the disclosure consequence, the funding route (if any), and the next action.
