# Expert engagement letter template

[Date]

[Expert name]
[Expert affiliation]
[Address]

**Re:** [Matter caption], [case or file number] — engagement for [precise disputed proposition]

Dear [Expert]:

## 1. Role and scope

You are engaged as a [consulting / testifying] expert. The precise disputed proposition is:

> [One sentence.]

The bounded questions are:

1. [Question 1.]
2. [Question 2.]
3. [Question 3.]

Do not opine beyond these questions without a written amendment.

## 2. Materials to review

- [Document, report, transcript, exhibit, or data set.]
- [Version and SHA-256 for each.]
- [Location and production reference.]

A versioned hash list is attached. If a source changes, stop and notify counsel before continuing; the claim and any dependent output must be revalidated.

## 3. Method and limits

- The method or discipline: [method].
- The applicable standard or protocol: [standard, edition, section, or UNVERIFIED].
- The limits on the opinion: [limits].
- The questions that require another discipline: [questions].

Do not supply a transfer, persistence, timing, causation, or offense conclusion the record and your method do not support.

## 4. Deliverables and deadline

- A written report or memorandum by [date].
- A preliminary call by [date].
- A review protocol: [what you will review, in what order, and what you will record].
- Any draft or supplemental report must be identified as such.

## 5. Role and disclosure consequence

- Consulting: [state the privilege and work-product position and the verified trigger, if any].
- Testifying: [state the report and disclosure obligations for the forum, or mark them UNVERIFIED].

A change in role requires a written amendment and a new disclosure analysis.

## 6. Preservation and retesting

Do not consume, alter, or destroy any sample or original medium without the preservation/retesting review in `evidence-matrix-admissibility/references/preservation-retesting.md`. The review must be complete and approved before any destructive procedure.

## 7. Compensation and independence

- Compensation: [rate and terms].
- No contingent fee tied to the outcome.
- Independence: [any relationship, conflict, or prior work].
- Confidentiality: [terms].

## 8. Acceptance

Accepted by:

_________________________
[Expert], [date]

Approved by:

_________________________
[Counsel], [date]

## Attachments

- Bounded question list.
- Versioned source and hash list.
- Claim register or evaluation record.
- Disclosure matrix, if the role is testifying.
- Preservation/retesting decision, if any sample may be consumed.
