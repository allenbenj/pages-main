# Issue-category checklist

Use with `legal-reasoning-framework` step 3 and orchestrator step 3, after the first issue tree exists. The checklist confirms that the tree has looked at each of the seven issue categories. It finds issues; it does not decide them. Every issue it surfaces still needs cited record facts (K6) and verified authority (R1, K1). Source: the issue-mapping categories in the Review Items import (`docs/REVIEW_ITEMS_ADOPTION.md`).

| # | Category | What to look for | Skills |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Criminal law | The elements of each charged and lesser included offense, and the record proof of each; statutory defenses; sufficiency of the charging document and any variance from the proof; sentencing exposure | `criminal-defense-strategy`, `prosecution-case-review`, `legal-research-verification` |
| 2 | Civil law | Civil claims and defenses arising from the same events (for example, malicious prosecution, abuse of process, defamation); claim deadlines and notice requirements; damages | `civil-rights-1983-strategy`, `settlement-negotiation`, `local-rules-deadlines` |
| 3 | Constitutional claims | Search, seizure, warrant and probable-cause defects; statements and double jeopardy; counsel, confrontation and speedy trial; state constitutional analogs | `criminal-defense-strategy`, `motion-brief-drafting`, `civil-rights-1983-strategy` |
| 4 | Procedural errors | Missed or misapplied deadlines; notice and service defects; jurisdiction and venue; joinder; jury selection and instructions; objections not preserved | `local-rules-deadlines`, `appellate-preservation`, `chronology-timeline-builder` |
| 5 | Evidentiary challenges | Foundation and authentication; hearsay; chain of custody; expert reliability; unfair prejudice against probative value; missing originals | `evidence-matrix-admissibility`, `transcript-impeachment`, `witness-examination` |
| 6 | Prosecutorial misconduct indicators | Brady, Giglio and Napue issues; late or missing disclosure; witness coaching or changed testimony; fabricated, altered or destroyed evidence; improper argument; charging irregularities. Search for these actively (`integrated-reasoning-for-motions.md`, adaptation 2). In a filing, each allegation is tied to cited record evidence (`master-rules.md` K6) | `discovery-brady-giglio`, `prosecution-case-review`, `transcript-impeachment`, `analysis-frameworks.md` section 8 |
| 7 | Due process violations | Fundamental-fairness defects: suppression of favorable evidence, use of false testimony, investigative failures, prejudicial delay, burden shifting, an unfair trial or a biased tribunal | `discovery-brady-giglio`, `criminal-defense-strategy`, `appellate-preservation`, `civil-rights-1983-strategy` |

## Procedure

1. Compare the issue tree's branches with the seven categories.
2. For each category, record the branch IDs that cover it. If none does, record "checked; none found" with the sources and searches used.
3. Add a branch, with its owner skill, for each supported issue that the tree is missing.
4. Expect overlap; one fact can support categories 6 and 7. Keep each argument in one branch and cross-reference the evidence (`analysis-frameworks.md` section 5, step 5).

## Self-check

- All seven categories have a recorded result.
- Every added branch names its owner skill and its record support or proof gap.
