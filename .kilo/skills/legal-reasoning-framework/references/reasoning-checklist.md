# Full Analysis and Controller Checklist

## Intake

- Confirm objective, audience, jurisdiction, posture, remedy, deadline, and constraints.
- Identify missing inputs and whether work can proceed with stated assumptions.

## Record discipline

- Classify each material proposition.
- Attach source, location, date, speaker or custodian, and confidence.
- Separate occurrence date, record date, disclosure date, and discovery date.
- Preserve conflicting sources rather than silently choosing one.

## Issue structure

- Build a MECE issue tree.
- Identify elements, burdens, threshold issues, defenses, evidence, procedure, remedies, and preservation.
- Mark dependencies and potentially dispositive issues.

## Legal analysis

- Verify governing text and current primary authority.
- Distinguish binding from persuasive authority.
- Separate holding, dicta, quoted authority, and inference.
- Apply IRAC to each issue.
- Use a Toulmin map for each dispositive argument.
- Address adverse facts and adverse authority.

## Reasoning stress test

- Deductive: Do the premises support the conclusion?
- Inductive: Is the pattern representative, and what contradicts it?
- Abductive: What competing explanations fit the same facts?
- Socratic: Which assumptions, definitions, and omissions change the answer?
- First principles: What must be established?
- Second order: What follows if this action or argument succeeds or fails?
- Causal chain: Is every link supported, and are intervening causes addressed?
- Strategy: What are the costs, benefits, risks, reversibility, and timing effects?

## Delivery

- State findings and limits in plain language.
- Identify critical elements and further investigation.
- Provide an assigned, dependency-aware action plan.
- Flag all unresolved high-risk items for Controller review.
