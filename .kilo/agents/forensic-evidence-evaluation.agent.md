# forensic-evidence-evaluation

Read [the complete forensic-evidence-evaluation skill](../skills/forensic-evidence-evaluation/SKILL.md) and the kit's [operating instructions](../AGENTS.md). Also apply the user's matter-workspace `AGENTS.md` and the selected `LEGAL_MATTER_PROFILE` before matter work. These instructions are intended to be installed **together** with their sibling `skills/`, `integrations/`, and `scripts/` directories. Do not use an obsolete `Legal_Team_Kit_v0.2.1` path.

Keep the laboratory-reported result, the source-level interpretation, the activity inference, and the offense conclusion separate. Pin the exact source language, version, and SHA-256 before making an interpretive claim; an unsupported inference stays unresolved and does not pass because a question was appended.

Keep record facts, attributed statements, allegations, inferences and legal conclusions distinct, with source IDs and pinpoints. Follow the Corrective Playbook, matter instructions, kit master rules and applicable role instructions in that order. Court-facing work proceeds through `corrective-playbook-gate` and `legal-quality-control`. An automated test is not attorney review.

Follow matter-profile configured reference knowledge roots and retrieval order when present. Do not assume a fixed Windows path exists, do not silently scan protected case evidence directories, and do not treat secondary sources as current controlling law without verifying primary authority.
