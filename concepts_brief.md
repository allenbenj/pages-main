# Two-Tier Information Architecture, Forensic Timeline, and Contradiction Hub Restructure

* **Author:** allenbenj
* **Date:** 2026-11-01

## Overview

"An Edifice of Lies" (*State v. Benjamin Allen*) presents court records, surveillance video, interview audio, and contradiction analysis on a static GitHub Pages website. Today, visitors face two structural problems:

1. **First-time visitors have no clear path through the case.** Landing on [`index.html`](file:///D:/web_page/pages-main/index.html) shows two competing top navigation bars (the shared project navigation bar stacked above a leftover Webflow mega-menu) and 20 pages split across four dropdown menus. When visitors open [`timeline.html`](file:///D:/web_page/pages-main/assets/pages/timeline.html), they see a zig-zag layout of rubber-stamp graphics (`False.webp`) and screenshot inserts rather than a clear comparison between what witnesses claimed and what the timestamped bar surveillance video shows.
2. **Advanced reviewers cannot find or cross-reference records in one place.** The 40 contradiction and impeachment cards live inside [`documentspage.html`](file:///D:/web_page/pages-main/assets/pages/documentspage.html) (titled "Documents" in the menu and "Catch All" in the browser tab) instead of [`contradictions.html`](file:///D:/web_page/pages-main/assets/pages/contradictions.html). Meanwhile, site search ([`site-search.js`](file:///D:/web_page/pages-main/shared/site-search.js)) only filters by broad file format (`page`, `pdf`, `media`, `image`) rather than by witness, legal issue, or timeline phase.

This proposal restructures the site into a two-tier architecture while keeping the dark charcoal, teal, and gold color scheme (`#111415` background, `#2c5a61` / `#a6ced2` teal accents, `#d4b85c` gold highlights) and the elapsed-arrest clock on [`index.html`](file:///D:/web_page/pages-main/index.html):
* **Tier 1 (Guided Narrative for First-Time Visitors):** A single navigation bar and a 4-step guided reading sequence (`Overview & Seven Factors` → `Forensic Timeline` → `Contradictions Hub` → `Evidence & Official Misconduct`) with a shared static step-progress bar at the top and bottom of those pages.
* **Tier 2 (Forensic Explorer & Source Archive for Researchers):** A consolidated Contradictions Hub on [`contradictions.html`](file:///D:/web_page/pages-main/assets/pages/contradictions.html), a dedicated Source Documents & Audio Archive on [`documentspage.html`](file:///D:/web_page/pages-main/assets/pages/documentspage.html), and faceted search across [`site-search.js`](file:///D:/web_page/pages-main/shared/site-search.js) and [`evidence-viewer.js`](file:///D:/web_page/pages-main/shared/evidence-viewer.js) filterable by witness/player, legal issue, timeline phase, and media type.

## Analysis

### 1. UX, Mobile Layout & Visual Hierarchy (`egm-design` & `egm-comprehension`)
* **Competing Navigation on [`index.html`](file:///D:/web_page/pages-main/index.html):** Lines 38–89 render `<div class="home-nav-shell"><div class="nav-tabs">`, while lines 90–185 render `<div class="nav">` (the legacy Webflow menu). These two bars use different labels for the same destinations (`Case Record` vs. `Framework`, `Evidence` vs. `Evidence Record`). Removing `<div class="nav">` eliminates 95 lines of duplicate navigation chrome while leaving the hero `InJustice_80_percent.webp` panel and `#elapsedClock` styles untouched.
* **Why [`timeline.html`](file:///D:/web_page/pages-main/assets/pages/timeline.html) Fails Visually and Forensically:**
  * The current page alternates `.timeline-event.left` and `.timeline-event.right` across a center spine, placing a decorative `False.webp` image (`.liar-stamp`) in the middle and a screenshot image (`.hug-lie-image`) on the opposite side.
  * Readers must zig-zag left and right to figure out which box is the camera timestamp and which box is the witness claim. Worse, the actual text of the contradicted statements is baked into flat `.webp` images that cannot reflow on mobile screens, cannot be read by screen readers, and cannot be indexed as HTML text. Furthermore, Event 05 (`12:41 AM — Love & Morrison leave Schooly's`, [`timeline.html#L148`](file:///D:/web_page/pages-main/assets/pages/timeline.html#L148)) reuses the wrong graphic file (`Peter_West_Leaves_Alone.webp`, copied from the `12:35 AM` event).
  * **Forensic Replacement:** Group the 20 events into four chronological phases of the night (`Phase I: Early Evening & Bar Arrival 6:30 PM–12:35 AM`, `Phase II: Initial Contact & Keys Dispute 12:41 AM–12:54 AM`, `Phase III: Escalation Inside & Outside Schooly's 1:12 AM–2:04 AM`, `Phase IV: Departure, Missing Hour & Hospital Arrival 2:20 AM–3:45 AM`) with a sticky phase jumper in `.page-rail`. Render each event as a 3-column `.timeline-event` forensic row:
    1. **Column 1 — Timestamp & Exhibit (`.event-meta`):** Monospace `.event-time` badge, exhibit badge (`Exhibit A`–`Exhibit Q` where applicable), and phase tag.
    2. **Column 2 — Surveillance / Factual Record (`.event-record`, Teal accent):** `.event-description` stating what the timestamped video or record shows, paired with an on-demand inline video player (`preload="none"`) and the `.video-btn[data-modal-image]` fullscreen modal button.
    3. **Column 3 — Contradicted Claim / Testimony (`.event-claim`, Amber/Red callout):** Plain-HTML transcription (`.event-claim-body`) of the contradicted testimony or interview statement as the primary text, with a secondary button (`.graphic-btn[data-modal-image]`) to open the original graphic insert in the modal.
  * **Mobile Collapse & Sticky Bar Hierarchy:** At viewports `<= 900px`, the 3-column grid collapses into a single vertical stack in strict reading order: **1. Timestamp & Exhibit → 2. Surveillance Record & Inline Video → 3. Contradicted Claim HTML Text (with secondary graphic modal button)**. To prevent competing sticky bars on `timeline.html`, the 4-step `.guided-path-bar` renders as a non-sticky banner at the top and bottom of `<main>`, while `.page-rail` (containing the Phase I–IV jump chips) is the sole sticky in-page bar below `.nav-tabs`.

Each of the 20 chronological rows is `<article class="timeline-event" data-phase="..." data-exhibit="...">` containing the three columns (`.event-meta`, `.event-record`, `.event-claim`) as children, preserving `.event-time`, `.event-description`, and `.video-btn[data-modal-image]` inside each `.timeline-event`. Extend `event_metadata()` in `sync_timeline_events.py` to extract `phase` (`data-phase`), `exhibit` (`data-exhibit`), `claimHtml` (`.event-claim-body` inner HTML), and `insertGraphic` (`.graphic-btn[data-modal-image]`), and add a `--self-test` mode that validates extraction against a 3-column fixture before editing `timeline.html`.

### 3. Performance, Media Preload & Quota Economics (`egm-implementation-readiness` & `egm-zoom-out`)
* **Strict `preload="none"` & Single-Active-Player Policy for Both Video and Audio:**
  * [`timeline.html`](file:///D:/web_page/pages-main/assets/pages/timeline.html) links 14 surveillance video clips across Exhibits A–I, L, M, O, P, and Q (letters J, K, and N are not linked on `timeline.html`).
  * The 40 cards moving to [`contradictions.html`](file:///D:/web_page/pages-main/assets/pages/contradictions.html) contain 45+ embedded `<audio>` elements (`carmen-love-audio`, `lauren-interview-audio`, and card excerpts).
  * Both pages must enforce `preload="none"` on every `<video>` and `<audio>` element so zero media bytes are fetched during initial page load. Inline video elements on `timeline.html` only set `video.src` when the user clicks "Play Inline", and a shared document-level `play` event listener (`document.addEventListener('play', ..., true)`) automatically pauses any other playing `<audio>` or `<video>` element on the page.
* **Search Index Payload & Facet UI:**
  * [`assets/search-index.json`](file:///D:/web_page/pages-main/assets/search-index.json) is fetched only when the user opens the search overlay. Adding granular records for the 40 contradiction cards and 20 timeline events with facet arrays (`players`, `issues`, `phase`, `kind`) adds ~25–35 KB uncompressed (~6–8 KB gzipped).
  * In [`shared/site-search.js`](file:///D:/web_page/pages-main/shared/site-search.js), add **three `<select>` filter dropdowns** (`Player / Witness`, `Legal Issue`, `Timeline Phase`) to `.site-search-toolbar` alongside the existing `Kind` filter chips to keep the UI and JS footprint minimal.
* **Release Validation Contract ([`tools/site_release.py`](file:///D:/web_page/pages-main/tools/site_release.py)):** Every `href`, `src`, `poster`, `data-modal-image`, and `data-href` across all staged HTML and CSS files must resolve to an existing file in the staged artifact, and total artifact size must remain under **900 MiB** (`943,718,400` bytes, enforced by `MAX_ARTIFACT_BYTES = 900 * 1024 * 1024` in [`tools/site_release.py#L19`](file:///D:/web_page/pages-main/tools/site_release.py#L19); currently ~680 MiB).

## Alternatives Weighed

1. **Keep `timeline.html` as a center-spine zig-zag and only restyle the CSS boxes:** Rejected because the core readability problem is structural. Alternating left/right cards with `False.webp` rubber stamps and text locked inside `.webp` images prevents side-by-side comparison of "What Was Testified" vs. "What the Camera Recorded" and breaks text search, screen readers, and mobile reading.
2. **Leave the 40 contradiction cards on `documentspage.html` and just add links from `contradictions.html`:** Rejected because naming a page "Documents" (`documentspage.html`) while filling its first 1,300 lines with contradiction analysis cards is the main reason users cannot tell `contradictions.html`, `documentspage.html`, and `evidence.html` apart.
3. **Convert the whole site into a single-page React application:** Rejected because the vanilla HTML architecture has zero build step for main pages, loads individual pages without a large JS bundle, and is enforced by Python maintenance scripts (`sync_nav.py`, `sync_timeline_events.py`, `sync_contradiction_cards.py`, `site_release.py`).
4. **Preload surveillance videos or interview audio on page load:** Rejected because preloading 14 video clips on `timeline.html` or 45+ audio clips on `contradictions.html` saturates bandwidth and delays interactivity; enforcing `preload="none"` and single-active-player playback gives instant on-demand playback without upfront network cost.
